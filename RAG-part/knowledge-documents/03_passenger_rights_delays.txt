DOCUMENT: DGCA PASSENGER CHARTER OF RIGHTS
AUTHORITY: Ministry of Civil Aviation & DGCA, Government of India
CATEGORY: Passenger Rights, Flight Delays, Cancellations, Denied Boarding, and Refunds
SOURCE: Official DGCA Passenger Charter (CAR Section 3, Series M)
SOURCE URL: https://civilaviation.gov.in/

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW AND SCOPE
==================================================

The Passenger Charter published by the Ministry of Civil Aviation defines the statutory rights of air travelers in India. 

These rules apply to all scheduled domestic flights operating within India and international flights originating from India, subject to DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR Section 3, Series M).

The rules govern airline responsibilities for:
- Flight delays.
- Flight cancellations.
- Denied boarding due to overbooking.
- Refund procedures and statutory deductions.
- Flight diversions.
- Passenger baggage loss and damage.
- Facilities for passengers with disabilities.
- Grievance escalation mechanisms (AirSewa).

==================================================
2. FLIGHT DELAYS AND PASSENGER RIGHTS
==================================================

When a flight is delayed, passenger entitlements depend on the scheduled flight duration (block time) and length of the delay:

SCENARIO 1: PASSENGERS CHECKED IN ON TIME
Airlines must provide free-of-charge meals and refreshments matching waiting time when delay reaches:
- 2 hours or more: for flights having a block time of up to 2.5 hours.
- 3 hours or more: for flights having a block time between 2.5 hours and 5 hours.
- 4 hours or more: for all other flights.

*Block time is the total flight duration measured from aircraft pushback from departure gate to arrival at destination gate.

SCENARIO 2: DOMESTIC DELAY EXCEEDING 6 HOURS
If a domestic flight is expected to be delayed by more than 6 hours:
- Rescheduled departure time must be communicated to passengers more than 24 hours prior to original departure.
- The airline must offer passengers an alternate flight departing within 6 hours OR a 100% full ticket refund.

SCENARIO 3: OVERNIGHT OR EXTENDED DELAYS
If the airline announces a delay more than 24 hours prior, and the delay exceeds 24 hours (or exceeds 6 hours for flights scheduled to depart between 20:00 and 03:00 hours):
- The airline must provide free hotel accommodation and airport transfers.

==================================================
3. FLIGHT CANCELLATIONS AND COMPENSATION
==================================================

Airlines must inform passengers of scheduled flight cancellations at least 2 weeks before the scheduled departure time and provide an alternate flight or refund acceptable to the passenger.

SCENARIO 1: NOTICE LESS THAN 2 WEEKS UP TO 24 HOURS
If cancellation is communicated less than 2 weeks but up to 24 hours before departure:
- The airline must offer an alternate flight departing within a reasonable timeframe or issue a full refund.

SCENARIO 2: LAST MINUTE CANCELLATION (LESS THAN 24 HOURS NOTICE OR NO NOTICE)
If passengers are not informed 24 hours in advance, or if they miss a connecting flight booked under the same PNR/ticket number:
The airline must provide an alternate flight OR a full refund PLUS statutory financial compensation:
- Block time up to 1 hour: Compensation of Rs. 5,000 OR booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge (whichever is less).
- Block time between 1 and 2 hours: Compensation of Rs. 7,500 OR booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge (whichever is less).
- Block time exceeding 2 hours: Compensation of Rs. 10,000 OR booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge (whichever is less).

ADDITIONAL CANCELLATION CONDITIONS:
- Meals and refreshments must be provided free of charge while waiting for alternate flights.
- Financial compensation is not payable if the passenger failed to provide valid contact details (mobile number/email) at booking.
- If the airline moves the flight to an alternate airport/terminal, the airline must bear transfer costs unless the passenger was informed at least 6 hours in advance.

==================================================
4. DENIED BOARDING (OVERBOOKING)
==================================================

Overbooking occurs when the number of confirmed passengers reporting on time exceeds the available seats.

PROCEDURE:
1. The airline must first ask for voluntary passengers to surrender their seats in exchange for negotiated benefits.
2. If forced denied boarding occurs against a passenger's will:

COMPENSATION ENTITLEMENTS:
- Alternate flight within 1 hour: No financial compensation is payable if an alternate flight departing within 1 hour is arranged.
- Alternate flight within 24 hours: 200% of booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge, up to a maximum of Rs. 10,000.
- Alternate flight after 24 hours: 400% of booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge, up to a maximum of Rs. 20,000.
- Passenger declines alternate flight: 100% full ticket refund PLUS 400% of booked one-way basic fare plus fuel charge, up to a maximum of Rs. 20,000.

==================================================
5. TICKET REFUND POLICIES AND CHARGES
==================================================

REFUND TIMELINES:
- Cash payments: Refund must be processed immediately at the airport ticket counter.
- Credit / Debit Card payments: Refund must be completed within 7 business days.
- Travel Agent / Portal bookings: Refund is routed through the booking agent.

STATUTORY TAX REFUNDS:
- If a passenger cancels a ticket, airlines MUST refund all statutory taxes, User Development Fees (UDF), Airport Development Fees (ADF), and Passenger Service Fees (PSF).
- This statutory tax refund applies to all ticket categories, including non-refundable, promotional, and special discounted fares.

CREDIT SHELL POLICY:
- Holding refunds in a credit shell is strictly the passenger's choice and cannot be imposed as a default practice by the airline.
- Airlines cannot charge an administrative fee for processing refunds.

24-HOUR "LOOK-IN" CANCELLATION AND CORRECTION WINDOW:
- Passengers can cancel or amend a ticket with zero cancellation or modification charges within 24 hours of booking, provided travel date is at least 7 days away.
- Airlines cannot levy any fee for correcting genuine passenger name spelling errors if notified within 24 hours of booking.

==================================================
6. FLIGHT DIVERSIONS
==================================================

When a flight is diverted to an unscheduled airport:
- Waiting time under 2 hours: Refreshments, water, tea/coffee and regular status announcements must be provided on board or at the terminal.
- Waiting time over 2 hours: The airline must immediately arrange disembarkation of passengers at the nearest airport with appropriate meals and facilitation.

==================================================
7. BAGGAGE LOSS, DAMAGE, AND DELAYS
==================================================

DOMESTIC CARRIAGE LIABILITY:
- Lost, damaged, or delayed checked baggage: Carrier liability is capped at Rs. 20,000 per passenger.
- Cargo loss or damage: Carrier liability is capped at Rs. 350 per kilogram.

INTERNATIONAL CARRIAGE LIABILITY:
- Baggage liability: Limited to 1,131 Special Drawing Rights (SDR) per passenger under the Montreal Convention.
- Cargo liability: Limited to 19 SDR per kilogram.

CONDITIONS:
- Compensation is payable if the damage/loss occurred while checked baggage was in airline custody.
- For unchecked hand baggage, the airline is liable only if damage resulted from its own fault or staff negligence.

==================================================
8. SPECIAL ASSISTANCE FOR PASSENGERS WITH DISABILITIES
==================================================

- No airline can deny carriage to persons with disabilities or reduce mobility, nor refuse to transport assistive aids, mobility equipment, escorts, or certified guide dogs.
- Passengers should notify special requirements at least 48 hours prior to scheduled departure.
- If accompanied by an escort, airlines must make reasonable efforts to assign adjacent seating.
- Assistive devices within India must be provided without any additional fee.
- If carriage is denied on safety grounds, the airline must state the precise reasons in writing.

==================================================
9. ESCALATION AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
==================================================

PASSENGER GRIEVANCE PROCESS:
1. Help Desks: File initial claims at airline help desks at departure/arrival terminals.
2. AirSewa: If unresolved by the airline, register a grievance on the Ministry of Civil Aviation's official AirSewa portal (www.airsewa.gov.in) or mobile application.
3. Legal Remedies: If unsatisfied with AirSewa resolution, passengers retain the right to approach consumer forums or statutory courts.

FORCE MAJEURE (EXCLUSIONS):
Airlines are exempt from paying delay/cancellation compensation if disruptions are caused by extraordinary circumstances beyond airline control, including:
- Natural disasters, extreme meteorological conditions, floods.
- Air traffic control (ATC) restrictions or directives.
- Security emergencies, political instability, strikes, or civil unrest.
