1. OVERVIEW OF DOMESTIC TRAVINSURE
============================================================

Domestic TravInsure is a travel insurance programme designed for
domestic passengers travelling with SpiceJet.

The insurance programme is offered by Tata AIG General Insurance
Company Ltd.

It provides coverage for certain travel-related risks, including:

- Accidental death and dismemberment.
- Emergency accidental medical expenses.
- Emergency medical evacuation.
- Checked baggage loss.
- Flight delay.
- Trip cancellation.
- Trip interruption.
- Emergency assistance services.

IMPORTANT:
Coverage is subject to the specific policy wording, exclusions,
deductibles, limits and conditions.

The insurance FAQ is not a substitute for the actual insurance
certificate or policy document.


============================================================
2. ELIGIBILITY
============================================================

According to the SpiceJet Domestic TravInsure FAQ:

- The policy is intended for passengers travelling domestically
  within India on SpiceJet.
- Indian domestic passengers can purchase the policy.
- Foreign passport holders may purchase it if they hold a valid
  work permit in India and are travelling domestically within India.
- Minimum age: 2 years on the date of travel.
- Maximum age: 70 years on the date of travel.

The actual eligibility must be confirmed against the policy
applicable to the passenger's booking.


============================================================
3. POLICY VALIDITY
============================================================

Domestic TravInsure provides coverage for a maximum period of
30 days from the policy commencement date.

The policy commencement date is the date the journey begins
on SpiceJet.

For a one-way journey:
- Coverage may continue for up to 30 days from the travel date,
  subject to the policy conditions.

For a round-trip journey:
- Coverage ends when the passenger returns to the originating
  city or when the 30-day validity period ends, whichever
  applicable condition is reached according to the policy.

For journeys involving other modes of transport:
- Certain benefits, such as trip cancellation and flight delay,
  are restricted to SpiceJet flights.
- Other benefits may apply during the policy validity period,
  subject to the policy wording.


============================================================
4. INSURANCE BENEFITS AND COVERAGE LIMITS
============================================================

The SpiceJet FAQ provides the following summary of Domestic
TravInsure benefits:

Benefit 1: Accidental Death and Dismemberment
Maximum coverage: Rs. 7,50,000
Coverage period: 24 hours, subject to policy conditions.

This benefit provides compensation in the event of accidental
death or covered dismemberment.

In case of death, the benefit is payable to the insured person's
legal heir.

Benefit 2: Emergency Accidental Medical Reimbursement
Maximum coverage shown in benefit table: Rs. 1,00,000.

IMPORTANT SOURCE CONFLICT:
The FAQ's benefit table states Rs. 1,00,000, while its explanatory
paragraph mentions Rs. 7,50,000.

The correct limit cannot be established from the FAQ alone.
Refer to the passenger's actual policy schedule and wording.

This benefit reimburses eligible medical expenses resulting
from a covered accident during the trip.

Benefit 3: Emergency Medical Evacuation and Repatriation
Maximum coverage: Rs. 5,00,000.

This benefit relates to eligible emergency transportation
and repatriation expenses, subject to policy conditions.

Benefit 4: 24-Hour Emergency Assistance
Coverage: Included.

The policy provides access to emergency assistance services.

Benefit 5: Trip Cancellation
Maximum coverage: Up to the original cost of the SpiceJet flight.

This benefit may reimburse eligible flight costs if a trip
must be cancelled for a reason covered by the policy.

Covered reasons may include:
- Unforeseen illness.
- Injury.
- Death involving the insured person.
- Illness, injury or death involving an eligible immediate
  family member or travel companion.

The complete list of covered reasons must be checked in the
policy wording.

Benefit 6: Checked Baggage Loss
Maximum coverage: Rs. 7,500.

The FAQ states that baggage-loss coverage applies when the
entire baggage is lost while in the custody of the common carrier.

Partial loss or individual missing items should not automatically
be treated as covered.

Benefit 7: Flight Delay
Maximum coverage: Rs. 1,500 per six-hour delay period,
up to a maximum of Rs. 10,500.

The benefit is subject to the delay conditions and requirements
in the policy wording.

Benefit 8: Common Carrier Delay
Maximum coverage: Rs. 1,500 per six-hour delay period,
up to a maximum of Rs. 10,500.

Benefit 9: Trip Interruption
Maximum coverage: Up to the original cost of the flight.

This benefit may cover eligible expenses when a trip must be
interrupted for a reason covered by the policy.

IMPORTANT:
All benefit amounts are specific to the Domestic TravInsure
FAQ version. They must not be assumed to apply to every
SpiceJet insurance product or booking.


============================================================
5. TRIP CANCELLATION
============================================================

Trip cancellation coverage is not an unrestricted cancellation
or refund facility.

The benefit applies only when the cancellation reason is
covered by the insurance policy.

Examples of potentially covered circumstances include:

- Unforeseen illness.
- Accidental injury.
- Death of the insured person.
- Death or qualifying illness/injury involving an eligible
  immediate family member or travel companion.

Pre-existing medical conditions and complications arising
from them are excluded according to the FAQ.

The customer must check the policy wording to confirm:

- Whether the reason is covered.
- Which documents are required.
- The maximum payable amount.
- Applicable deductibles and exclusions.

The airline's normal ticket cancellation rules and the
insurance claim process are separate processes.


============================================================
6. TRIP INTERRUPTION
============================================================

Trip interruption coverage may reimburse eligible costs
up to the original cost of the flight.

It applies only when the trip is interrupted because of
a reason covered by the policy.

The customer should refer to the actual policy wording
for covered circumstances, documentation and claim limits.

Do not promise reimbursement without confirming eligibility.


============================================================
7. FLIGHT DELAY
============================================================

The FAQ lists the following flight-delay benefit:

- Rs. 1,500 for each qualifying six-hour delay period.
- Maximum benefit: Rs. 10,500.

The benefit is subject to the policy's definition of a
qualifying delay and other conditions.

Important distinctions:

- An airline delay does not automatically guarantee
  an insurance payout.
- The delay must satisfy the policy conditions.
- Required supporting documents may be necessary.
- The insurance benefit is separate from any compensation
  or assistance available under aviation passenger-rights rules.

Customers should retain their booking confirmation and
official flight-delay communication.


============================================================
8. BAGGAGE LOSS
============================================================

The FAQ lists a maximum checked-baggage-loss benefit
of Rs. 7,500.

The FAQ specifically states that the entire baggage must
have been lost while in the custody of the common carrier.

This means:

- Complete baggage loss may qualify, subject to the policy.
- A missing item inside otherwise delivered baggage should
  not automatically be treated as covered.
- Delayed baggage and damaged baggage are separate situations.
- The actual policy wording determines the final coverage.

Recommended customer action:

1. Report the missing baggage to the airline immediately.
2. Obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) or equivalent
   official baggage report.
3. Keep the baggage tag and boarding pass.
4. Preserve receipts and other supporting documents.
5. Contact Tata AIG to understand the claim requirements.


============================================================
9. MEDICAL EMERGENCY AND ACCIDENTS
============================================================

The policy provides benefits for eligible accidental
medical expenses and emergency medical evacuation.

If a passenger experiences an accident during the covered trip:

1. Seek immediate medical assistance.
2. Contact the insurance provider using the official
   assistance or claims contact details.
3. Keep hospital records and medical bills.
4. Preserve prescriptions, diagnostic reports and
   other supporting documents.
5. Follow the insurer's claim procedure.

Medical expenses are reimbursed only when they satisfy
the policy conditions.

IMPORTANT EXCLUSIONS:

- Pre-existing medical conditions are not covered according
  to the FAQ.
- Complications arising from pre-existing conditions are
  also excluded.
- Sports and similar activities are not covered under
  this Domestic TravInsure policy, according to the FAQ.

Do not assume that every illness, injury or medical expense
is covered.


============================================================
10. SPORTS AND ADVENTURE ACTIVITIES
============================================================

The SpiceJet Domestic TravInsure FAQ states that sports
and similar activities are not covered.

Passengers participating in sports or adventure activities
should not assume that accidents during those activities
will qualify for reimbursement.

The actual policy wording must be checked for the complete
list of excluded activities.


============================================================
11. CONNECTING FLIGHTS AND OTHER TRANSPORT
============================================================

The FAQ distinguishes between SpiceJet flights and other
transport arrangements.

Trip cancellation and flight-delay benefits are restricted
to SpiceJet flights.

Other eligible benefits may remain applicable during the
policy validity period, even when the passenger uses another
mode of transport for part of the journey.

The exact applicability depends on the policy conditions.

Do not assume that every connecting flight operated by
another airline is covered for delay or cancellation.


============================================================
12. POLICY CANCELLATION AND REFUND
============================================================

According to the Domestic TravInsure FAQ:

- The policy is non-cancellable.
- The insurance premium is non-refundable.
- The policy cannot be changed once issued, even if the
  passenger postpones or extends the trip.

These statements refer to the specific Domestic TravInsure
policy described in the FAQ.

Do not confuse an insurance-premium refund with a flight-ticket
refund.

Flight-ticket cancellation and insurance-premium refund
are separate matters.


============================================================
13. CHANGE IN TRAVEL DATE
============================================================

The Domestic TravInsure FAQ states that the policy cannot
be changed once issued.

However, it also explains that when SpiceJet reschedules
a return flight, the insurance automatically changes to
the new flight if the rescheduled flight is within the
specified 30-day policy period.

The customer should confirm the policy status with Tata AIG
when travel dates or flight arrangements change.

Do not guarantee coverage after a voluntary flight change
or trip extension.


============================================================
14. CERTIFICATE OF INSURANCE
============================================================

Passengers are encouraged to carry a copy of their
Certificate of Insurance while travelling.

The FAQ states that successful processing should be reflected
on the SpiceJet confirmation page and itinerary.

If the passenger has not received the certificate or has
lost it, they can contact Tata AIG.

The passenger may need to provide:

- Certificate number, if available.
- Full name.
- SpiceJet flight booking number.
- Travel dates.
- Date of insurance purchase.

The insurer can use these details to help locate the policy
and provide a copy, subject to verification.


============================================================
15. CLAIM PROCESS
============================================================

For an insurance claim:

Step 1:
Identify the type of claim:
- Medical emergency.
- Accidental death or dismemberment.
- Baggage loss.
- Flight delay.
- Trip cancellation.
- Trip interruption.
- Emergency evacuation.

Step 2:
Contact Tata AIG using its official support channels.

Step 3:
Explain the incident and provide the policy or certificate
number, if available.

Step 4:
Collect the documents requested by the insurer.

Examples of supporting documents may include:

- Certificate of Insurance.
- Flight ticket and booking confirmation.
- Boarding pass.
- Government-issued identity document.
- Medical reports and hospital bills.
- Death certificate, where applicable.
- Baggage irregularity report.
- Airline delay or cancellation confirmation.
- Receipts and proof of expenses.

The required documents depend on the claim type.

Step 5:
Submit the claim and supporting documents according
to the insurer's instructions.

Step 6:
Follow up with Tata AIG using the claim reference number.


============================================================
16. CLAIM PROCESSING TIME
============================================================

The SpiceJet Domestic TravInsure FAQ states:

- Claims are processed within 14 days after Tata AIG
  receives all required documentation.
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days from the expiry
  of the policy.

These are the timelines stated on the FAQ page.

Actual processing may depend on document completeness,
claim verification and applicable policy requirements.

Customers should confirm current claim deadlines directly
with Tata AIG.

============================================================

According to the FAQ, the following limitations apply:

1. Pre-existing medical conditions are not covered.
2. Complications arising from pre-existing conditions
   are not covered.
3. Sports and similar activities are not covered.
4. Trip cancellation applies only to specified covered reasons.
5. Flight-delay and trip-cancellation benefits are restricted
   to SpiceJet flights.
6. Checked-baggage-loss coverage requires the entire baggage
   to have been lost while in the custody of the carrier.
7. The policy has a maximum validity period of 30 days,
   subject to the return-journey condition.
8. The policy cannot be cancelled or changed once issued,
   subject to the stated rescheduling condition.

The complete exclusions must be obtained from the actual
policy wording.

19. CUSTOMER SUPPORT DECISION GUIDELINES
============================================================

If the customer asks:
"Does my travel insurance cover flight delays?"

Respond:
The Domestic TravInsure FAQ lists a benefit of Rs. 1,500
per qualifying six-hour delay period, up to Rs. 10,500.
The payout depends on the policy conditions and qualifying
delay requirements.

If the customer asks:
"Will I get my money back if I cancel my trip?"

Respond:
Trip cancellation insurance may cover eligible cancellation
reasons such as specified illness, injury or death-related
emergencies. It does not cover every voluntary cancellation.
The actual policy wording determines eligibility.

If the customer asks:
"Does travel insurance cover pre-existing diseases?"

Respond:
The Domestic TravInsure FAQ states that pre-existing
conditions and complications arising from them are not covered.

If the customer asks:
"Is lost baggage covered?"

Respond:
The FAQ lists checked-baggage-loss coverage up to Rs. 7,500.
It specifies that the entire baggage must have been lost
while in the custody of the common carrier. The claim is
subject to policy conditions.

If the customer asks:
"How can I file an insurance claim?"

Respond:
Contact Tata AIG through its official support channels.
Keep your Certificate of Insurance, flight details and
supporting documents ready.

If the customer asks:
"Can I cancel my insurance separately?"

Respond:
The Domestic TravInsure FAQ describes the policy as
non-cancellable and the premium as non-refundable.
Please confirm your specific policy conditions with Tata AIG.

If the customer asks:
"Is my connecting flight covered?"

Respond:
The FAQ restricts trip cancellation and flight-delay benefits
to SpiceJet flights. Other benefits may apply during the
policy validity period, subject to the policy wording.

If the customer asks:
"What is my exact insurance coverage?"

Respond:
Please check your Certificate of Insurance and policy schedule.
Coverage limits and conditions depend on the policy issued
for your booking.

============================================================

1. Identify the insurance provider and exact policy before
   answering coverage questions.

2. Do not mix Tata AIG Domestic TravInsure with other
   SpiceJet insurance products.

3. Do not mix domestic and international insurance policies.

4. Never promise that a claim will be approved.

5. Never describe travel insurance as a guaranteed
   flight-ticket refund.

6. Always distinguish between:
   - Flight cancellation by the airline.
   - Voluntary ticket cancellation by the passenger.
   - Insurance-based trip cancellation.
   - Insurance-premium refund.

7. If the customer asks about a specific claim, request
   the relevant policy and incident details.

8. If the policy wording is unavailable or a coverage
   condition is unclear, direct the customer to Tata AIG.

9. Do not invent coverage amounts, deductibles, exclusions,
   waiting periods or claim deadlines.

10. If the FAQ contains conflicting information, disclose
    the conflict and ask the customer to verify the policy
    schedule.

11. Do not treat the FAQ as a replacement for the actual
    Certificate of Insurance.

12. Escalate disputed claims and complex insurance questions
    to the insurer or a human support representative.
