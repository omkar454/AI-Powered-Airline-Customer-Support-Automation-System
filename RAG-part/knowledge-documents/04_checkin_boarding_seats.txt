==================================================
1. OVERVIEW OF CHECK-IN
==================================================

Check-in is the process through which a passenger confirms their intention to travel on a booked flight, completes the required formalities and receives a boarding pass.

IndiGo provides several check-in methods:

- Web check-in through the IndiGo website.
- Mobile check-in through the IndiGo application.
- Kiosk check-in at supported airports.
- Airport counter check-in.
- Smart check-in, where available.

Passengers generally need a valid booking reference (PNR) and the required identification documents.

Completing check-in does not guarantee boarding. Passengers must also complete baggage drop, security screening and boarding formalities within the applicable deadlines.

==================================================
2. WEB CHECK-IN TIME WINDOWS
==================================================

DOMESTIC FLIGHTS:

- Web check-in opens 48 hours before scheduled departure.
- Web check-in closes 60 minutes before scheduled departure.

INTERNATIONAL FLIGHTS:

- Web check-in opens 24 hours before scheduled departure.
- Web check-in closes 75 minutes before scheduled departure.

Passengers should complete web check-in within the applicable time window.

The availability of online check-in may depend on the route, itinerary, passenger category and booking conditions.

Official page:
https://www.goindigo.in/web-check-in.html

==================================================
3. HOW TO COMPLETE WEB CHECK-IN
==================================================

General procedure:

1. Visit the official IndiGo website.
2. Select the Check-in option.
3. Enter the booking reference (PNR).
4. Enter the passenger's last name or other requested booking details.
5. Retrieve the booking.
6. Select the passenger who needs to check in.
7. Complete the required declarations.
8. Select a seat if available.
9. Complete the check-in process.
10. Download, print or receive the boarding pass using the available options.

Passengers carrying checked baggage must proceed to the airport baggage drop counter after completing online check-in.

==================================================
4. WEB CHECK-IN ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Standard web check-in may not be available for certain passenger categories.

Examples include:

- Unaccompanied minors (UNMR).
- Passengers travelling with a medical case (MEDA).
- Passengers travelling on a stretcher (STCR).
- Certain bookings made using international credit or debit cards.
- Certain connecting itineraries where the connecting flight falls outside the permitted check-in window.

Passengers who cannot complete online check-in should contact IndiGo or use airport counter check-in, subject to the applicable deadlines.

==================================================
5. MOBILE / APP CHECK-IN
==================================================

Mobile check-in allows passengers to complete check-in using the IndiGo mobile application.

General procedure:

1. Open the IndiGo mobile application.
2. Retrieve the booking using the PNR and requested passenger details.
3. Select the passenger.
4. Select a seat if available.
5. Complete the required declarations.
6. Confirm check-in.
7. Access the boarding pass through the application or available delivery options.

Mobile check-in allows passengers to complete check-in without visiting an airport counter, provided they meet the eligibility requirements.

Official information:
https://www.goindigo.in/app-check-in.html

==================================================
6. SMART CHECK-IN
==================================================

Smart Check-in is a contactless check-in facility offered by IndiGo where available.

Passengers can check in through supported digital channels and receive their boarding pass through available delivery methods, such as WhatsApp or email.

Published timing guidance:

- Domestic flights: up to 60 minutes before departure.
- International flights: up to 75 minutes before departure.

Availability and delivery options may depend on the booking and registered contact details.

Smart Check-in does not remove the requirement to meet baggage drop, security and boarding deadlines.

==================================================
7. KIOSK CHECK-IN
==================================================

Kiosk check-in is a self-service facility available at supported airports.

General procedure:

1. Visit an IndiGo self-service kiosk.
2. Enter the booking reference (PNR).
3. Provide the requested passenger information.
4. Select the passenger.
5. Select a seat if available.
6. Complete the required declarations.
7. Confirm check-in.
8. Collect the printed boarding pass.

Kiosk availability depends on the airport.

Passengers should complete kiosk check-in before the applicable airport check-in deadline.

Official information:
https://www.goindigo.in/kiosk-check-in.html

==================================================
8. AIRPORT COUNTER CHECK-IN
==================================================

Passengers can complete check-in at IndiGo airport counters, subject to the applicable airport and flight deadlines.

DOMESTIC FLIGHTS:

- Check-in counters close 60 minutes before scheduled departure.
- IndiGo recommends arriving at the airport at least 2 hours before departure.

INTERNATIONAL FLIGHTS:

- Check-in counters close 75 minutes before scheduled departure.
- IndiGo recommends arriving at the airport at least 4 hours before departure.

Passengers should allow sufficient time for:

- Check-in.
- Baggage drop.
- Security screening.
- Immigration formalities, where applicable.
- Boarding gate procedures.

Passengers arriving after counter closure may be denied check-in.

==================================================
9. CHECKED BAGGAGE AFTER ONLINE CHECK-IN
==================================================

Passengers who have completed online check-in but are carrying checked baggage must proceed to the designated IndiGo baggage drop counter.

Published deadlines:

- Domestic flights: baggage must be handed over within the applicable 60-minute pre-departure deadline.
- International flights: baggage must be handed over within the applicable 75-minute pre-departure deadline.

Completing online check-in does not extend the baggage drop deadline.

For baggage weight limits, cabin baggage and excess baggage charges, refer to:

==================================================
10. BOARDING PASS
==================================================

A boarding pass is a document or electronic credential issued after successful check-in.

It generally contains:

- Passenger name.
- Flight number.
- Travel date.
- Origin and destination.
- Boarding gate, when assigned.
- Boarding time.
- Seat number, when assigned.

Passengers can access their boarding pass after completing check-in.

IndiGo provides facilities to view, download or print boarding passes through its website and supported digital channels.

Official page:
https://www.goindigo.in/boarding-pass.html

Passengers should carry a printed or accessible electronic boarding pass, subject to airport requirements.

A boarding pass does not guarantee boarding. Passengers must still carry valid identification, complete security checks and reach the boarding gate on time.

==================================================
11. AIRPORT IDENTIFICATION DOCUMENTS
==================================================

Passengers must present valid identification documents according to IndiGo's Conditions of Carriage and applicable government regulations.

Examples of accepted identification for Indian domestic travel include:

- Passport.
- Voter photo identity card.
- Aadhaar or m-Aadhaar issued by UIDAI.
- PAN card.
- Driving licence.
- Government-issued service photo identity card.
- Student photo identity card issued by a government or government-recognised educational institution.

Different requirements may apply to infants, children and foreign nationals.

For international journeys, passengers must carry the passport, visa and other documents required by the destination and transit countries.

Official source:
https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html

==================================================
12. BOARDING GATE DEADLINE
==================================================

IndiGo's published Conditions of Carriage states that boarding gates close 25 minutes before scheduled departure.

Passengers must reach the boarding gate before the applicable gate closure time.

Important instructions:

- Monitor airport display screens.
- Check boarding gate announcements.
- Follow instructions from airport and airline staff.
- Proceed to the gate early.
- Do not assume that the gate remains open until the scheduled departure time.

Passengers arriving after gate closure may be denied boarding.

==================================================
13. SEAT SELECTION
==================================================

IndiGo provides assigned seating.

Passengers may select seats through:

- Advance seat selection during booking.
- Booking management after reservation.
- Web check-in.
- Mobile check-in.
- Airport kiosk, where available.
- Airport check-in counter, where available.

IndiGo's Seat Select service allows passengers to choose seats in advance, subject to availability and applicable charges.

Seat selection fees depend on the seat type, flight and applicable fee schedule.

Passengers should review the seat map and displayed charges before confirming a paid seat.

Seat assignments may be changed by the airline for operational or safety reasons.

==================================================
14. EMERGENCY EXIT ROW SEATS
==================================================

Emergency exit row seats are subject to additional safety requirements.

Passengers occupying these seats must:

- Be at least 15 years old on the date of travel.
- Be physically and mentally capable of assisting during an emergency.
- Understand and follow crew instructions.
- Be capable of operating or assisting with the emergency exit.
- Not be pregnant.
- Not have a condition that prevents them from performing the required emergency duties.

Passengers who do not meet the applicable safety conditions cannot occupy emergency exit row seats.

Airline staff may reassign passengers when required for safety reasons.

==================================================
15. SEAT ASSIGNMENT FOR FAMILIES
==================================================

Passengers travelling together may select seats during booking or check-in, subject to availability and applicable charges.

Passengers should not assume that all members of a booking will automatically receive adjacent seats.

Passengers travelling with children, infants or requiring special assistance should contact IndiGo in advance.

Seat assignments remain subject to aircraft configuration and safety requirements.

==================================================
16. FAILURE TO CHECK IN OR ARRIVE ON TIME
==================================================

Passengers must complete check-in, baggage drop and boarding formalities within the applicable deadlines.

Failure to:

- Complete check-in on time.
- Hand over checked baggage before the deadline.
- Present valid travel documents.
- Reach the boarding gate before closure.

may result in denial of boarding or treatment as a no-show under the Conditions of Carriage.

The consequences depend on the booking, fare conditions and applicable airline policy.

Passengers should not assume that missing a flight automatically qualifies them for a full refund.

==================================================
17. SPECIAL ASSISTANCE
==================================================

Passengers requiring medical assistance, mobility assistance, stretcher services or other special arrangements should contact IndiGo before travelling.

Certain passenger categories may not be eligible for standard online check-in and may need assistance at the airport counter.

Examples include:

- Unaccompanied minors.
- Passengers with medical cases.
- Passengers travelling on stretchers.
- Passengers requiring special assistance.

Passengers should follow the airline's instructions and arrive early enough to complete the required formalities.

==================================================
18. CUSTOMER SUPPORT TROUBLESHOOTING
==================================================

PROBLEM: Passenger cannot complete web check-in.

Suggested guidance:

- Verify the PNR and passenger details.
- Confirm that the check-in window is open.
- Check whether the passenger belongs to a restricted category.
- Check whether the itinerary contains connecting flights.
- If online check-in remains unavailable, contact IndiGo or use airport counter check-in before the deadline.

PROBLEM: Passenger cannot download the boarding pass.

Suggested guidance:

- Confirm that check-in was completed successfully.
- Retrieve the booking through the official boarding-pass facility.
- Enter the required booking details.
- Check the registered email or mobile delivery channel.
- Contact IndiGo or airport staff if the problem continues.

PROBLEM: Passenger wants to change their seat.

Suggested guidance:

- Retrieve the booking through IndiGo's website or application.
- Open the seat-selection facility.
- Review available seats.
- Check the displayed fees.
- Confirm the selection after reviewing the applicable charges.

PROBLEM: Passenger has checked in but has checked baggage.

Suggested guidance:

- Proceed to the designated baggage drop counter.
- Hand over baggage before the applicable deadline.
- Do not assume that web check-in extends the baggage drop deadline.

PROBLEM: Passenger missed boarding.

Suggested guidance:

- Confirm the scheduled departure and gate closure time.
- Direct the passenger to IndiGo airport staff.
- Explain that a boarding pass does not guarantee boarding after gate closure.
- Do not promise a refund or free rebooking without checking the applicable policy.

==================================================
19. CUSTOMER SUPPORT RESPONSE GUIDELINES
==================================================

For check-in questions, identify:

- Domestic or international journey.
- Flight date and departure time.
- Whether the passenger has a valid PNR.
- Whether the passenger has connecting flights.
- Whether the passenger has checked baggage.
- Whether the passenger belongs to a special assistance category.

For boarding-pass questions, identify:

- Whether check-in was completed.
- Whether the passenger can access the PNR.
- Whether the passenger needs to download, print or retrieve the boarding pass.

For seat-selection questions, identify:

- Whether the passenger wants a standard or preferential seat.
- Whether the booking is already confirmed.
- Whether the requested seat is available.
- Whether an additional fee applies.

The RAG system should provide policy information only.

It must not claim to:
- Complete passenger check-in.
- Issue a boarding pass.
- Reserve a seat.
- Modify a booking.

These actions require confirmation from an authorized airline system or API.
